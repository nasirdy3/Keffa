What is KEFA?

KEFA (Kebbi eFootball Arena) is the premier digital platform for competitive eFootball mobile gaming in Birnin Kebbi. We organize professional leagues, tournaments, and community events.

How do I join a tournament?

Register an account and create your Team Profile. Then navigate to the Tournaments page, select an active tournament, and click 'Join Now'.

How are payments handled?

We support Paystack for instant verification and bank transfers for offline payments. Offline payments require manual Admin verification.

How is 'Player of the Week' chosen?

It's automated! The Captain of the team with the most wins and best goal difference over the last 7 days wins the badge.

What happens if my opponent doesn't show up?

Our Auto-Loss system forfeits the match in your favor if your opponent fails to mark themselves as 'Ready' within the scheduled window.

Can I reschedule a match?

Yes, use the 'Request Postponement' feature in your dashboard. Your opponent must accept, and an Admin must approve the new time.

How do I play Friendly Matches?

Go to the Community Chat and click "Create Challenge". Share your game code, and once accepted, it tracks in your history.
