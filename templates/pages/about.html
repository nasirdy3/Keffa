About KEFA

The Vision of Digital Football in Birnin Kebbi

Our Mission

KEFA (Kebbi eFootball Arena) is a complete digital world built for eFootball gamers in Birnin Kebbi, Kebbi State. Think of it as the Premier League meets Esports Arena.

There is a growing community of gamers who love Konami’s eFootball mobile game. Before KEFA, matches were scattered in WhatsApp groups and random chats. Now, we have a professional home where every gamer can exist, compete, shine, and build a legacy.

How It Works

🌟 The Player

Every user becomes a "Player" with a verified profile, stats, and history.

🛡️ The Team

Create your personal brand (e.g., Musa FC) and compete for glory.

🏆 Tournaments

Leagues, Cups, and the e-Champions League with automated fixtures.

📹 Highlights

Upload match highlights to verify scores and build your fame.
